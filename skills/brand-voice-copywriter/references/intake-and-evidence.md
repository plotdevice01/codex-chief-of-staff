# Intake and evidence

## Required intake

Record answers without improving them during intake.

- Business identity: legal name, public name, locations, ownership, and approved description.
- Offer: products or services, price rules, delivery model, eligibility, exclusions, and current availability.
- Audience: buyer, user, influencer, objections, decision context, and reading level.
- Position: category, alternatives, differentiators, proof, and claims the client refuses to make.
- Voice material: approved copy, leadership examples, customer language, anti-examples, and visual guidelines.
- Channels: website and paid media. Add press and social. Add video and lifecycle messages. Record sales material and event use.
- Governance: package and claim owners. Add privacy and finance owners. Record media and rights owners. Add the release owner and any other required role.
- Measurement: business action, channel metric, baseline, test window, and decision rule.

Use the blank templates in `assets/client-workspace/_templates/`.

## Source classes

Classify every source before synthesis.

| Class | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| Approved internal | Current record approved by its owner | Facts and controlled language within its scope |
| Authoritative external | Current law or regulator rule. Also current platform policy and primary research. | General constraints with a review date |
| Observed public | Current public page or published asset | Evidence of current usage, not proof of internal truth |
| Transcript | Spoken material with a named source and date | Aggregate language patterns unless quotation rights are recorded |
| Aspirational | Desired personality or future position | Direction only; not a factual claim |
| Inference | Analyst conclusion from one or more sources | Label as inference and route for adoption |
| Unknown | Missing or conflicting information | Add to the gap list and do not fill |

## Source priority

Use the narrowest current source that owns the fact. Prefer approved internal records for client facts. Use current primary external sources for laws and platform rules. Use the current technical standard when it applies. Recheck any source that can change.

If two sources conflict, preserve both records and route the conflict to the named owner. Do not average them into a new statement.

## Transcript use

Extract repeated sentence length, message order, verbs, emotional register, and terminology. Keep counts separate from interpretation. Do not use one speaker as the corporate voice unless the client adopts that decision.

Do not publish raw transcript text until the speaker and rights scope are approved. Record the intended channel and exact quotation in that decision.

## Evidence ledger

Each material statement needs:

- A stable source ID.
- The exact source location.
- Source class and owner.
- Review date.
- Allowed use and known limit.

Write claims into the claim register before drafting copy. If support is incomplete, mark the row `Hold`.
