# 03_voice

One job: turn approved evidence into the corporate voice architecture.

## Inputs

- Working: `../02_evidence/output/voice-evidence.md`
- Working: `../02_evidence/output/source-register.csv`
- Working: `../01_intake/output/client-intake.md`
- Template: `_templates/voice-architecture.md`
- Template: `_templates/terminology-register.csv`

## Process

1. Draft a corporate voice that is separate from any spokesperson.
2. Define principles, failure signs, message order, and tone settings.
3. Build preferred and prohibited terminology from the evidence record.
4. Label every inference or proposal.

## Outputs

- `voice-architecture.md`
- `terminology-register.csv`
- `adoption-decisions.md`

## Human check

The package owner records a decision for each proposed voice rule.
