# Strategy and copy

## Inputs

Approved intake, evidence, brand voice, and production-system reference.

## Process

Choose the smallest useful slide count. Draft at least three cover hooks and the complete carousel. Make Slide 2 reopen curiosity, use one job per slide, deliver a real payoff, and end with one action. Run AI Sloppy Copy and repair failures.

## Outputs

Completed `copy.md` plus slide roles in `run.json`. Add the exact copy to the manifest. Include the caption and alt text draft.

## Human check

Approve the exact strategy and copy version. Do not start full image production.
