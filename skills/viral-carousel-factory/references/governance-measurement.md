# Governance and measurement

## Evidence gate

Classify every material claim before writing it into a slide:

- `Verified`: supported by an approved source that directly matches the claim.
- `Client asserted`: supplied by the client but not independently verified.
- `Heuristic`: a production judgment or source recommendation, not a guaranteed fact.
- `Unknown`: unsupported or missing. Do not publish it as fact.

Record the source and scope. Include the date checked plus approved wording. High-stakes medical or legal claims require current authoritative evidence and appropriate client review. Apply the same gate to financial or housing claims. Hiring and safety claims use it too.

Visual examples do not prove performance. Likes shown in a mockup, a creator's self-reported follower count, or an unattributed statistic is not evidence for a client claim.

## Rights and disclosure

- Confirm permission for logos, photos, likenesses, testimonials, screenshots, fonts, and client-supplied assets.
- Record organic and paid use separately for each person or likeness. Record AI editing and AI generation reference permission separately too.
- Use references to describe visual properties. Do not redistribute source files or imitate protected signatures and brand identifiers.
- Record whether AI-generated or edited imagery requires disclosure under client policy, law, contract, or platform rules.
- When a material connection exists, place a clear disclosure with the endorsement. Do not bury it behind a link or at the end of a long caption.
- Never invent a testimonial, endorsement, quote, credential, result, or product interface.

## Release gate

A run can become `release_ready` only when:

- strategy copy and anchor approvals are recorded;
- final files match the manifest count and order;
- copy accuracy and visual consistency pass;
- claims and citations pass;
- rights and disclosure decisions are recorded;
- every used asset is approved in the asset register and every depicted person has the required likeness permissions;
- alt text and caption are complete;
- live platform route and maximum item count are recorded;
- the platform verification date is recorded;
- release approval is recorded.

Publication remains a separate authorized action.

## Measurement

Do not call a carousel viral because it looks good. Compare it against a defined baseline and campaign goal.

Record when available:

- reach and impressions;
- saves and shares per reached account;
- completion or final-slide view rate when the platform exposes it;
- profile actions and clicks;
- qualified messages or leads;
- revenue tied to the CTA;
- hook and cover for the tested version;
- topic plus slide count;
- route and publish time.

Change one major variable per experiment when practical. Cover tests should preserve the same core content. Record results and sample size before promoting a heuristic to a client rule.
