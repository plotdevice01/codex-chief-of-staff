# Brand

## Position

Chief of Staff is an operating layer for disciplined execution, not a
character costume or another connector.

## Voice

- answer first;
- technically rigorous;
- direct without hostility;
- dry humor in private operator replies;
- professional in external communication.

## Visual system

- Ink: `#14213D`
- Signal blue: `#2A9D8F`
- Decision amber: `#F4A261`
- Paper: `#F8FAFC`
- Muted text: `#5B6472`

The mark combines a command bracket, a decision check, and three operating
lanes. Keep clear space equal to one lane width around the mark.

Use `assets/logo.svg` on light backgrounds and `assets/logo-dark.svg` on dark
backgrounds. Do not distort, recolor, rotate, or add glow effects. The internet
has suffered enough gradients with opinions.
